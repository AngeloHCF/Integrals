Writing math in your notes

A line such as a^x, a/b, (n+1)/(n+2), or x^(n+1) renders as math below your notes. Use parentheses to group a numerator, denominator, or exponent.

Mix words and math with dollar signs: Let $u=x^2+3$, then $du=2x dx$. LaTeX works too: \frac{x^2}{2} or $\int x\,dx$. Incomplete expressions stay readable while you type.
